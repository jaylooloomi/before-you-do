### 繁體中文

- 管理／設定主視窗預設永遠置頂，避免被一般視窗遮住。

### English

- Keep the snippet manager/settings window always on top of normal windows.
- Reassert topmost status when opening from the picker or tray. Background sign-in startup still leaves the manager hidden.
