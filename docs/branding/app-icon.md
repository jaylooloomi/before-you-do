# before-you-do app icon

Concept: a mint snippet card entering an ivory conversation bubble, representing adding one more instruction before sending. The charcoal rounded tile keeps the mark legible on light and dark desktops.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, with a transparent exterior. The approved master is `src-tauri/icons/before-you-do-master.png`. `scripts/icons.py` exports its PNG, ICO, ICNS, tray RGBA and UI sizes without redrawing the artwork.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final desktop application icon for before-you-do, a local keyboard shortcut tool that lets users choose a saved instruction and paste it into an AI conversation before sending. Draw ONE polished professional app icon, not a presentation sheet or mockup. Core symbol: a large soft ivory speech bubble with a short tail at bottom left, with a smaller mint green snippet card sliding into the bubble from the upper right, overlapping its edge; two bold dark horizontal strokes inside the mint card represent a saved instruction. Bubble contains one dark bold text stroke lower left so the insertion reads clearly. Communicate 'add one more instruction before sending', not searching, AI magic, messaging delivery, or checklists. Extremely simple distinct geometric silhouette, high contrast, broad rounded shapes and generous breathing room; it must remain legible at 16, 24 and 32 pixels. Rounded square charcoal navy base with a subtle very restrained satin depth, contemporary macOS desktop icon craft, clean restrained colors mint teal and ivory; no busy gradients, no glass reflections, no neon glow, no sparkles, no magnifying glass, no checkmark, no robot, no arrow, no keyboard, no letters, no text, no badges, no border stroke around the base. Square composition, front-facing, centered, icon occupies 90 percent of canvas. Exterior outside the rounded square must be genuinely transparent. No cast shadow outside the tile. Crisp edges, production-ready logo clarity. Output a single 1024x1024 icon.

The generator returned a 1280×1280 master; exports are derived from that original.
